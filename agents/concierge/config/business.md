# Palmgrove Stays — business profile

> **Fictional business** created for the Switchboard portfolio demo. Any resemblance to a real
> company is unintended. Policies are modelled on typical North Goa holiday-rental operations;
> no real guest data is used.

**What we are:** a small holiday-rental company managing four private villas in North Goa, India.
**Guests:** families, groups of friends, couples, remote workers on longer stays; domestic and
international.
**Team:** a guest-relations team (messages, 8am–10pm IST daily), an on-ground caretaker at each
villa, and a duty manager reachable 24/7 for emergencies.
**Booking channels:** our own website, plus major booking platforms. Bookings made on a platform
follow that platform's cancellation and payment terms where they differ from ours.
**Currency:** Indian Rupees (₹). Prices include GST unless stated.
**Tone:** warm, clear, practical. We never promise what the policy doesn't allow, and we hand
over to a person when a request needs judgement (refund exceptions, damage, complaints, safety).
