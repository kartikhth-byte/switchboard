# Studio Nine Salon (fictional demo business)

> Fictional salon for the Switchboard portfolio. All names, numbers and addresses are invented.

**Location:** 12 Fontainhas Lane, Panaji, Goa. Street parking only; the nearest paid lot is at
Panaji Market, 5 minutes' walk.
**Hours:** Tuesday–Thursday 10am–7pm, Friday 10am–8pm, Saturday 9am–8pm, Sunday 10am–5pm.
Closed Mondays.
**Payment:** UPI, cards and cash. Prices include GST.
**Stylists:** Anita (colour specialist), Farhan (men's cuts and beards), Leela (cuts, kids, colour).

**Policies**
- Please arrive 5 minutes early. More than 15 minutes late may mean a shorter service or a new slot.
- Cancel or reschedule free up to 4 hours before the appointment. Later cancellations of colour,
  highlights or keratin carry a ₹500 fee.
- Colour and keratin need a patch test at least 48 hours before; it takes 5 minutes, any open slot.
- Children under 12 must come with an adult.
- We use sulphate-free products; tell us about allergies when booking.
- Gift vouchers are sold at the front desk only.
- We don't do bridal, nails, waxing or spa services.
