# Children, pets and events

## Children
- Children are welcome at every villa except The Cashew House for children under 5 (open
  rooftop).
- Children under 12 can stay on extra mattresses at no charge at Casa Kokum and Villa Sol,
  up to 2 per booking. Children 12 and over count as adults for the guest limit.
- A cot and a high chair are available free at every villa; ask at least 3 days before arrival.
- Pools have no fences or lifeguard. Children must be supervised around water at all times.

## Pets
- Dogs are allowed at **Casa Kokum and Villa Sol only**, up to 2 dogs per booking, with a
  ₹1,500 per-stay cleaning fee.
- Please tell us about pets when booking. Pets that weren't declared may lead to the stay being
  ended without refund.
- Pets are not allowed in the pool or on beds and sofas, and must not be left alone in the villa.
- Cats and other animals aren't accepted, because the villas aren't secure for them.

## Events and parties
- Events of any kind (birthdays, wedding functions, parties) with more than 4 visitors need
  written approval from a manager **before booking**, and are only possible at Villa Sol.
- Approved events carry an event fee and a higher security deposit, set case by case.
- Events must end by 10:00 pm (see quiet hours in "House rules").
- Unapproved events may lead to the stay being ended without refund.

## Celebrations
Small celebrations for the booked guests are welcome. We can arrange a cake, flowers or
decorations with 48 hours' notice; prices on request.
