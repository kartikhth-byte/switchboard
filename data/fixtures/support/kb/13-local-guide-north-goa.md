# Local guide — North Goa

Our caretakers' favourite things near the villas. We don't take commissions from any of these.

## Beaches
- **Ashwem and Morjim** — quieter, wide beaches; Morjim is an olive ridley turtle nesting site
  from November to March, so keep away from marked nests.
- **Anjuna and Vagator** — cliffs, cafés and sunsets; busier in peak season.
- **Candolim** — long, easy beach with lifeguards in season; 5 minutes' walk from Monsoon Cottage.

## Markets
- **Anjuna flea market** — Wednesdays, from late October to April, 9am to sunset.
- **Mapusa market** — Fridays are the big day; best for spices, cashews and local sausages.
- **Saturday night markets** in Arpora — in peak season only.

## Food and drink
Ask the caretaker for current recommendations; restaurants change often. Try Goan dishes like
fish curry rice, xacuti, cafreal and bebinca. Feni is the local cashew spirit.

## Day trips
- **Fort Aguada and Chapora Fort** — 20–30 minutes from most villas.
- **Old Goa churches** (a UNESCO World Heritage Site) — about 45 minutes.
- **Spice farms near Ponda** — about 75 minutes; half-day tours with lunch.
- **Dudhsagar waterfalls** — a full day; access depends on season and official rules, so check
  before planning.
