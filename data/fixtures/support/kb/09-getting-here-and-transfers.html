Getting here and airport transfers

Airports

Goa has two airports:

  • Manohar International Airport, Mopa (GOX) in North Goa — the closest to our villas: about 35–45 minutes to Siolim and Assagao, 50 minutes to Anjuna and Candolim.
  • Dabolim Airport (GOI) in South Goa — about 75–90 minutes to our villas, longer in evening traffic.

Check which airport your flight uses before booking a transfer.

Trains

The closest railway stations are Thivim (about 25 minutes from Assagao and Siolim) and Karmali. We can arrange a pickup from Thivim for ₹1,200.

Getting around

  • Taxis: use the GoaMiles app or ask the caretaker to call a local taxi.
  • Scooter and car rental: the caretaker can recommend licensed rental shops. A valid driving licence is required; international guests need an International Driving Permit.
  • Parking: every villa except Monsoon Cottage has private parking for 2 cars. Monsoon Cottage has street parking only.

Finding the villa

The exact address and a map pin are sent 48 hours before check-in, after the balance is paid.
