# Prices, seasons and minimum stays

All prices are per villa per night in Indian Rupees and include GST. A ₹500 processing fee is
added once per booking.

## Nightly rates
| Villa | Standard (1 Mar – 30 Nov) | Peak (1 Dec – 28 Feb) | Festive (24 Dec – 2 Jan) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Villa Sol | ₹22,000 | ₹38,000 | ₹55,000 |
| Casa Kokum | ₹16,000 | ₹28,000 | ₹42,000 |
| The Cashew House | ₹9,000 | ₹16,000 | ₹24,000 |
| Monsoon Cottage | ₹4,500 | ₹7,500 | ₹11,000 |

## Monsoon discount
Stays with every night between 1 June and 30 September get **20% off** the standard rate.

## Minimum stays
- Standard season: 2 nights.
- Peak season: 3 nights.
- Festive period (24 Dec – 2 Jan): 5 nights.

## Longer stays
- 14 nights or more: 15% off the nightly rate at any villa.
- Monsoon Cottage monthly rate: ₹85,000 for 28 nights or more in the standard season. Not
  available in peak or festive periods.
- Long-stay and monthly discounts don't combine with the monsoon discount; the larger discount
  applies.

## What's included
Daily housekeeping (except Sundays), linen and towels, Wi-Fi, utilities and the caretaker. Meals,
airport transfers and extras are not included (see "Extras: chef, groceries and experiences").

## Price matching and discounts
We don't price-match booking platforms and don't offer discount codes over chat. Group or
corporate quotes for 3 or more villas are handled by a person.
