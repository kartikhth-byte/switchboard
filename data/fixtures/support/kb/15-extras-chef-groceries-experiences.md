# Extras: chef, groceries and experiences

Extras are added to your running bill and paid by UPI or card before check-out.

## Private chef
- A local cook can prepare Goan or Indian home-style meals in the villa.
- ₹2,500 per meal for up to 6 people, ₹3,500 for 7–10 people, plus the cost of ingredients.
- Book at least 24 hours ahead; breakfast must be booked by 6:00 pm the day before.
- Tell us about dietary needs when booking the chef.

## Grocery stocking
We can stock the kitchen before you arrive: send a list at least 48 hours before check-in. You
pay the shop price plus a ₹500 service charge.

## Breakfast hamper
A breakfast hamper (bread, eggs, fruit, butter, jam, coffee, tea, milk) is ₹800 for 2 people,
₹1,500 for up to 6.

## Experiences
- Spice farm tour with lunch: ₹2,200 per adult, including transport.
- Sunset river cruise from Siolim: ₹1,800 per adult (October to May only).
- Goan cooking class at the villa: ₹2,000 per person, minimum 2 people.
- Yoga session at the villa: ₹1,500 per session for up to 4 people.

## Not offered
We don't arrange alcohol deliveries, nightclub entry or scooter rentals ourselves; the caretaker
can recommend licensed providers.
