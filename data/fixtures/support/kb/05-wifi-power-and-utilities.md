# Wi-Fi, power and utilities

## Wi-Fi
- Every villa has fibre broadband with a backup 4G router. Typical speeds are 100 Mbps at Villa
  Sol and Casa Kokum, and 50 Mbps at The Cashew House and Monsoon Cottage.
- The network name and password are on a card by the TV and in your arrival message.
- If Wi-Fi drops, restart the white router in the hall cupboard (unplug for 30 seconds). If it is
  still down after 15 minutes, switch to the backup network "Palmgrove-Backup" and tell the
  caretaker.
- We can't guarantee speeds for video calls during heavy monsoon storms (June–September).

## Power cuts
- Short power cuts are common in Goa, especially in the monsoon.
- Every villa has an inverter that keeps lights, fans, Wi-Fi and the fridge running for about
  6 hours. Air conditioners, the water heater and the pool pump do **not** run on backup.
- Villa Sol and Casa Kokum also have a generator that starts automatically for longer cuts and
  runs everything except one AC per villa.

## Air conditioning
- All bedrooms have AC; living rooms have ceiling fans.
- Please keep doors and windows closed when the AC is on.

## Hot water
Each bathroom has a water heater. Switch it on about 20 minutes before a shower; the switch is
outside the bathroom door.

## Drinking water
Filtered drinking water is available from the kitchen purifier. Bottled water is not provided,
but the caretaker can buy it for you at cost.
