# Accessibility

We want every guest to know exactly what to expect before booking.

| Villa | Step-free entrance | Ground-floor bedroom with bathroom | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Villa Sol | Yes (ramp at side gate) | Yes, 1 bedroom | Grab rails in that bathroom; other bedrooms upstairs |
| Casa Kokum | No (3 steps) | Yes, 2 bedrooms | Portable ramp available on request |
| The Cashew House | No (5 steps) | No | Bedrooms and terrace reached by stairs only |
| Monsoon Cottage | Yes | Yes | Narrow bathroom door (70 cm) |

- None of the pools have hoists or ramps.
- Garden paths are gravel at Casa Kokum and Villa Sol.
- Assistance dogs are welcome at every villa, including those where pets aren't normally allowed,
  with no cleaning fee.

If you need a detailed measurement or have a specific requirement, ask to speak to a person
before booking and we'll answer honestly.
