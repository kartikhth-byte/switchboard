# Kitchen and appliances

## Every villa kitchen has
- Gas hob, microwave, toaster, electric kettle, fridge-freezer, mixer-grinder.
- Cookware, crockery and cutlery for the villa's maximum guest count, plus spares.
- Basics: salt, pepper, sugar, cooking oil, tea, coffee and dish soap.
- A water purifier for drinking water.

## Villa-specific
- Villa Sol and Casa Kokum also have an oven, a dishwasher and a coffee machine.
- The Cashew House and Monsoon Cottage have a two-burner hob; no oven or dishwasher.

## Barbecue
Villa Sol and Casa Kokum have a charcoal grill. Charcoal is ₹400 per bag from the caretaker.
Open fires elsewhere are not allowed (see "House rules").

## Gas cylinders
The hob runs on a gas cylinder with a spare connected. If the gas runs out, tell the caretaker —
don't change cylinders yourself.

## Other appliances
- TV with streaming apps in every living room (sign in with your own accounts and sign out before
  you leave).
- Hair dryer and iron with ironing board in every villa.
- Bluetooth speaker at every villa except Monsoon Cottage (remember quiet hours from 10:00 pm).
