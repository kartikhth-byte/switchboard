# Our villas

Palmgrove Stays manages four private villas in North Goa. Every villa is booked whole — we never
share a villa between separate bookings.

## Casa Kokum — Assagao
- 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, sleeps up to 6 adults (plus 2 children under 12 on extra mattresses).
- Private pool (8m × 4m), garden, outdoor dining for 8.
- About 15 minutes by car to Anjuna and Vagator beaches.
- Best for families and small groups who want a quiet base.

## Villa Sol — Siolim
- 4 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, sleeps up to 8 adults (plus 2 children under 12).
- Private pool, river-facing deck, indoor games room.
- About 10 minutes to Morjim beach, 20 minutes to Mopa airport road.
- Best for larger groups and longer stays; has a dedicated work desk in two bedrooms.

## The Cashew House — Anjuna
- 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, sleeps up to 4 adults.
- Plunge pool (4m × 2.5m), rooftop terrace.
- Walking distance (10 minutes) to Anjuna beach and the Wednesday flea market.
- Best for couples and friends. Not suitable for children under 5 because of the open rooftop.

## Monsoon Cottage — Candolim
- 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom, sleeps up to 2 adults.
- No pool; guests can use the pool at our partner hotel 300m away, 8am–7pm, at no charge.
- 5 minutes' walk to Candolim beach.
- Best for couples and solo remote workers; discounted monthly rates (see pricing).

## What every villa has
- Daily housekeeping (except Sundays), fresh linen and towels every 3 days.
- A caretaker on site or within 5 minutes, 8am–8pm.
- Fully equipped kitchen, filtered drinking water, washing machine.
- Wi-Fi and power backup — see "Wi-Fi, power and utilities".
