# Guest ID and registration

Indian law requires accommodation providers to check and record the identity of every adult
guest. We can't check anyone in without valid ID.

## Indian citizens
- Every guest aged 18 or over shows a government photo ID on arrival: Aadhaar card, passport,
  driving licence or voter ID.
- PAN cards are not accepted as ID for check-in.
- You can upload ID copies through the link in your confirmation email before arrival to make
  check-in faster.

## Foreign nationals
- Every foreign guest (any age) shows their **passport and valid Indian visa** (or OCI card) on
  arrival.
- We are required to submit a **Form C** registration to the immigration authorities within
  24 hours of your arrival. The caretaker takes photos of the passport photo page and visa page
  for this.
- Overseas Citizens of India (OCI) cardholders still need Form C registration.

## Why we ask and what we keep
- ID details are used only for the legal registration above and kept as long as the law
  requires. We don't share them for marketing.
- We never ask for ID details or payment details over chat. If someone asks you for these by
  message claiming to be from Palmgrove Stays, please contact us through our website.

## Guests under 18
Guests under 18 must stay with a parent or legal guardian who is on the booking. We can't accept
bookings where every guest is under 18.
