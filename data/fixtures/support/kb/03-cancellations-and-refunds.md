# Cancellations, changes and refunds

This policy applies to bookings made directly on our website. **Bookings made on a booking
platform follow that platform's cancellation terms.**

## Standard season (1 March – 30 November)
| When you cancel | Refund of amount paid |
|---|---|
| 30 or more days before check-in | 100% |
| 14–29 days before check-in | 50% |
| Less than 14 days before check-in | No refund |

## Peak season (1 December – 28 February)
Includes Christmas, New Year and the main winter season.

| When you cancel | Refund of amount paid |
|---|---|
| 45 or more days before check-in | 100% |
| 21–44 days before check-in | 50% |
| Less than 21 days before check-in | No refund |

Bookings that include **24 December to 2 January** are non-refundable once confirmed, but can
be moved once to other dates (see below).

## Changing dates
- You can move your dates **once** at no charge if you ask at least 14 days before check-in and
  the new dates are within 6 months. Any price difference for the new dates is payable.
- Shortening a stay after check-in is treated as a cancellation of the unused nights, with no
  refund for those nights.

## How refunds are paid
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7–10 working days.
- The ₹500 payment-processing fee is not refunded on cancellations.

## Exceptions
We do not make exceptions to this policy over chat. If something serious has happened (illness,
a flight cancelled by the airline, a family emergency), ask to speak to a person — a manager
reviews each case individually and will reply within one working day.
