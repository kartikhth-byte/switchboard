# House rules

These rules apply at every Palmgrove Stays villa. They are part of the booking terms.

## Guests and visitors
- Only guests named on the booking may stay overnight. The maximum guest count for each villa is
  in "Our villas".
- Day visitors are welcome between 9:00 am and 9:00 pm, up to 4 visitors at a time, and must be
  told to the caretaker. More visitors count as an event (see "Children, pets and events").

## Noise
- Quiet hours are **10:00 pm to 7:00 am**. Goa's rules restrict loudspeakers and amplified music
  outdoors after 10:00 pm, and our villas are in residential neighbourhoods.
- Music indoors at a conversational volume is fine at any time.
- After two noise complaints from neighbours, the duty manager may end the stay without refund.

## Smoking
- No smoking indoors, including e-cigarettes. Smoking is allowed on outdoor terraces and in the
  garden; please use the ashtrays provided.
- A ₹5,000 deep-cleaning charge applies if anyone smokes indoors.

## Damage and breakages
- Tell the caretaker about any breakage straight away. Small breakages (glasses, plates) are not
  charged.
- Damage beyond normal wear is deducted from the security deposit at repair cost, with photos.

## Saving water and power
Goa has water and power shortages in April and May. Please switch off the AC when you leave the
villa and avoid running hoses in the garden.

## Things that are not allowed
- Fireworks or open fires (barbecues in the provided grill are fine).
- Illegal drugs anywhere on the property. We will end the stay without refund.
- Moving furniture between rooms or outdoors.
