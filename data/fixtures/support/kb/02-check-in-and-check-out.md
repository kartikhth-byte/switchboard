# Check-in and check-out

## Times
- **Check-in:** from 2:00 pm.
- **Check-out:** by 11:00 am.
- Arrivals after 10:00 pm are fine but must be confirmed at least 24 hours ahead so the caretaker
  can meet you. Arrivals between midnight and 6:00 am carry a ₹1,500 late-arrival fee.

## Early check-in and late check-out
- Early check-in (from 10:00 am) and late check-out (until 2:00 pm) are each ₹1,000 per villa,
  and only possible when the villa is free the night before or after.
- We can confirm early check-in or late check-out no earlier than 48 hours before, because it
  depends on other bookings.
- Check-out later than 2:00 pm is charged as an extra night at the current nightly rate.

## On arrival
1. The caretaker meets you at the villa, checks ID for every adult guest (see "Guest ID and
   registration") and walks you through the house.
2. The security deposit is collected or confirmed (see "Payments and deposits").
3. You receive the Wi-Fi details, the caretaker's number and the duty manager's emergency number.

## On departure
- Leave keys on the kitchen counter, or hand them to the caretaker if they are on site.
- Please run the dishwasher or leave dishes in the sink — no need to clean.
- The caretaker checks the villa within 2 hours of check-out. The deposit is refunded within
  5 working days after that check (see "Payments and deposits").

## Luggage
We can hold luggage at the villa until 6:00 pm on your check-out day at no charge, as long as
it is collected by then.
