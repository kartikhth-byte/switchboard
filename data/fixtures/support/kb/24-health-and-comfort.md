# Mosquitoes, sun and staying comfortable

This page covers practical comfort tips. It isn't medical advice — for health questions, please
speak to a doctor or pharmacist.

## Mosquitoes
- Every bedroom has window screens and a plug-in repellent; refills are in the kitchen drawer.
- Mosquitoes are worst at dusk and during the monsoon. Close screens and doors in the evening.
- Mosquito nets are available on request for any bed.

## Sun and heat
- April and May are the hottest months; midday temperatures can exceed 35°C.
- Drink plenty of water — filtered drinking water is always available in the kitchen.

## Pharmacies and doctors
- The caretaker can direct you to the nearest pharmacy, usually within 10 minutes.
- A list of nearby 24-hour hospitals is on the fridge in every villa.
- In an emergency, call 112.

## Food safety
Drink filtered or bottled water rather than tap water. Our private chef uses filtered water for
cooking and washing produce.
