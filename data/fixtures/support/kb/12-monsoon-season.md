# Staying during the monsoon (June – September)

The monsoon brings heavy rain, green landscapes and fewer crowds. It's a lovely time for a quiet
stay if you know what to expect.

## What to expect
- Heavy rain most days, often in bursts with sunny breaks. July and August are the wettest months.
- Swimming in the sea is banned by local authorities for most of the season because of strong
  currents. Beach shacks are mostly closed.
- Power cuts and slower internet are more common (see "Wi-Fi, power and utilities").
- Roads near the villas can flood briefly after very heavy rain; the caretaker will advise on
  travel.

## What we do
- Villa pools stay open but may close for up to 24 hours after storms for cleaning.
- We provide umbrellas, raincoats for adults, and dehumidifiers in bedrooms.
- Housekeeping runs as normal; laundry may take an extra day to dry.

## Price
Stays with every night between 1 June and 30 September get 20% off the standard rate (see
"Prices, seasons and minimum stays").

## Weather and cancellations
The standard cancellation policy applies in the monsoon. Bad weather alone is not a reason for a
refund. If an official weather warning closes roads or airports on your arrival day, ask to speak
to a person and a manager will review moving your dates.

## Things to do in the monsoon
Waterfall visits (with a local guide), spice farm tours, Old Goa churches, cooking classes and
monsoon festivals such as São João in late June. See "Local guide: North Goa".
