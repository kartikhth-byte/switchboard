# Contact and support hours

| Who | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Chat assistant | 24/7 | Website chat |
| Guest-relations team | 8:00 am – 10:00 pm IST, every day | Website chat (ask for a person) or reply to any booking email |
| Caretaker (during your stay) | 8:00 am – 8:00 pm | Phone number in your arrival message |
| Duty manager (emergencies only) | 24/7 | Emergency number in your arrival message |

## Response times
- Guest-relations team: within 1 hour during support hours; messages sent overnight are answered
  by 9:00 am.
- Complaints: a manager replies within 4 hours during support hours (see "Problems during your
  stay, complaints and feedback").

## Emergencies
For fire, medical or police emergencies, call **112** first, then the duty manager.

## Languages
The team speaks English, Hindi, Konkani and Marathi.
