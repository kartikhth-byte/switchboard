# Booking safety and scams

Fake listings and payment scams are common for holiday rentals. Here's how to stay safe.

- We only take booking payments through our website's secure checkout or the booking platform you
  booked on. **We never ask for payment by personal bank transfer, gift cards or crypto.**
- We never ask for card numbers, OTPs, passwords or ID numbers over chat, WhatsApp or phone.
- Our emails come only from our website's domain. If a message asks you to pay somewhere else or
  "urgently" confirm card details, don't reply — contact us through our website.
- The villa address is sent 48 hours before check-in after the balance is paid; we don't share it
  earlier, to protect current guests.
- If you think you've been targeted by a scam using our name, ask to speak to a person and also
  report it to India's cyber-crime helpline 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
