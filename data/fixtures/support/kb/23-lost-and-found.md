# Lost and found

- The caretaker checks the villa after every check-out and logs anything left behind.
- Tell us as soon as possible what you left and where. We keep items for **30 days**.
- We can courier items within India at cost plus a ₹300 handling fee, paid in advance by UPI or
  card. International shipping is at cost plus ₹1,000 handling; we can't ship liquids, food,
  medicines or valuables over ₹20,000 internationally.
- Unclaimed items after 30 days are donated, except passports and ID documents, which are handed
  to the local police station.
- We're not responsible for valuables left outside the in-room safe.
