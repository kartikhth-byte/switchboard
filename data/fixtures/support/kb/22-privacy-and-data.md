# Privacy and your data

## What we collect
- Booking details: names, email, phone number, dates, payment status.
- ID details required by law at check-in (see "Guest ID and registration").
- Chat conversations, to answer your questions and improve the service.

## How we use it
Only to run your booking, meet legal requirements and respond to you. We don't sell personal
data, and we only send marketing emails if you opt in.

## Chat assistant
- The chat assistant is an AI system. A person reviews conversations that are passed to them.
- Card numbers and ID numbers should never be shared in chat. If you share them by mistake, they
  are masked in our logs.

## Cameras
CCTV covers the gate and driveway only. There are no cameras inside any villa.

## Your rights
You can ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted (except where the law requires us to
keep it, such as Form C records) by asking to speak to a person.
