# Housekeeping, linen and laundry

## Housekeeping
- Daily housekeeping between 10:00 am and 1:00 pm, **except Sundays**.
- Tell the caretaker if you'd like a different time that day or no cleaning at all.
- Housekeeping covers sweeping, mopping, bathrooms, bins and washing up left in the sink.

## Linen and towels
- Bed linen and bath towels are changed every 3 days, or between guests on a booking.
- Pool and beach towels are provided (2 per guest) and swapped daily on request.
- Extra linen or towels are free; ask the caretaker.

## Laundry
- Every villa has a washing machine and a drying rack; detergent is provided.
- Laundry service (washed, dried and folded by our team) costs ₹300 per load, returned the next
  day — an extra day in the monsoon.
- We don't offer dry cleaning, but the caretaker can arrange it with a local shop at cost.

## Rubbish and recycling
North Goa villages separate wet and dry waste. Please use the green bin for food waste and the
blue bin for dry waste; the caretaker handles collection.

## Pest control
Villas are treated for pests every month. Ants and geckos are normal in Goa. If you see
cockroaches or a snake, tell the caretaker straight away — don't try to handle a snake yourself.
