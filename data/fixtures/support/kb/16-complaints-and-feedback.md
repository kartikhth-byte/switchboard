# Problems during your stay, complaints and feedback

## Something isn't working
- Tell the caretaker first — most things (Wi-Fi, AC, hot water, a blocked drain) are fixed the
  same day.
- If it's urgent outside 8:00 am – 8:00 pm, contact the duty manager on the emergency number in
  your arrival message.
- If something essential (water, power, AC in all bedrooms) can't be fixed within 24 hours, a
  manager will offer options, which may include moving you to another villa or a partial refund
  for the affected nights.

## Making a complaint
- Message us any time, or ask the assistant to pass you to a person.
- A manager replies within **4 hours** between 8:00 am and 10:00 pm IST, or by 10:00 am the next
  morning for messages sent overnight.
- Decisions about refunds, compensation or exceptions are made by a manager, never by the chat
  assistant.

## Damage disputes
If you disagree with a deposit deduction, reply to the deduction email within 7 days with any
photos or details. A manager who wasn't involved in the original check reviews it.

## Feedback
After check-out we email a short feedback form. Feedback goes to the owners directly and is never
shared publicly without permission.
