# Long stays and remote work

## Discounts
- 14 nights or more: 15% off at any villa.
- Monsoon Cottage: ₹85,000 for 28 nights or more in the standard season (1 March – 30 November).
  See "Prices, seasons and minimum stays".

## Working from the villa
- Villa Sol has dedicated work desks in two bedrooms; the other villas have a dining table and a
  desk in the main bedroom.
- Fibre broadband with a 4G backup router at every villa. We can't guarantee video-call quality
  during monsoon storms.
- Power backup keeps Wi-Fi running for about 6 hours during cuts.

## Long-stay housekeeping
Housekeeping is daily except Sundays, as for all stays. For stays of 28 nights or more, a deep
clean is done every two weeks at no charge.

## Utilities on long stays
Electricity is included up to 30 units (kWh) per day at Monsoon Cottage on monthly stays; use
above that is charged at ₹10 per unit. Electricity is included without limit at the other villas.

## Visas and registration
Foreign guests on long stays still need Form C registration (see "Guest ID and registration").
We can't advise on visa rules; please check with the Indian authorities.
