# How to book and change a booking

## Booking
- Book on our website: choose the villa and dates, pay the 30% deposit, and you'll receive a
  confirmation email with your **booking reference** (it starts with "PG-").
- The chat assistant can answer questions but **can't make, change or cancel bookings**. It can
  look up an existing booking when you give the booking reference and the email used to book.

## Changing or cancelling
- Date changes and cancellations for direct bookings are done by our team. Ask the assistant to
  pass you to a person, or reply to your confirmation email.
- The change and cancellation rules are in "Cancellations, changes and refunds".

## Adding guests
You can add guests up to the villa's maximum (see "Our villas") at no extra charge. Tell us their
names before arrival; every adult needs ID at check-in.

## Bookings made on a booking platform
Contact us through that platform for changes, cancellations and payments. Its terms apply where
they differ from ours, and we can't see or change payments made there.

## Holds and waitlists
We don't hold dates without the deposit. For popular dates you can ask a person to add you to
the waitlist; we'll contact you if a cancellation frees the dates.
