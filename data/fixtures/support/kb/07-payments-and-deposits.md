# Payments and the security deposit

## Paying for your booking (direct bookings)
- **30% deposit** when you book, to confirm the dates.
- **Balance** due 14 days before check-in (21 days in peak season, 1 December – 28 February).
  If the balance isn't paid on time we send a reminder; after 3 days the booking may be cancelled
  under the cancellation policy.
- Bookings made less than 14 days before check-in are paid in full when booked.
- We accept UPI, Indian and international cards, and bank transfer. A ₹500 processing fee applies
  to each booking. We do not accept cash for booking payments.

## Security deposit
| Villa | Deposit |
|---|---|
| Villa Sol | ₹20,000 |
| Casa Kokum | ₹15,000 |
| The Cashew House | ₹10,000 |
| Monsoon Cottage | ₹5,000 |

- The deposit is taken as a card pre-authorisation or a UPI payment on arrival.
- It is released or refunded within **5 working days** after the caretaker's check-out check.
- Deductions are only made for damage beyond normal wear, missing items, indoor smoking
  (₹5,000), or late check-out beyond 2:00 pm (one night). We send photos and an itemised list
  before deducting anything.

## Invoices
A GST invoice is emailed after check-out. If you need it in a company's name with a GSTIN, tell us
before check-in — invoices can't be reissued afterwards.

## Extra charges during the stay
Items like early check-in, airport transfers or a private chef are added to a running bill and
settled by UPI or card before check-out.
