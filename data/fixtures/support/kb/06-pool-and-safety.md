# Pool use and safety

## Pool hours and rules
- Pools are open from 7:00 am to 10:00 pm. Pool lights switch off at 10:00 pm.
- There is no lifeguard. Children under 12 must be supervised by an adult at all times in and
  around the pool.
- No glass in or next to the pool — use the plastic cups provided.
- No diving: the pools are 1.4m deep at most.
- Pools are cleaned every morning between 7:00 and 8:00 am; please stay out while the caretaker
  is cleaning.

## Monsoon pool closures
During heavy rain or lightning, please leave the pool. The caretaker may close the pool for up
to 24 hours after a storm to clear debris and rebalance the water.

## Emergencies
- For a medical emergency, call **112** (India's national emergency number) first, then the duty
  manager.
- The nearest 24-hour hospitals are listed on the card on the fridge in every villa.
- Each villa has a first-aid kit under the kitchen sink and a fire extinguisher by the main door.

## Beach safety
- Many North Goa beaches have strong currents, especially from June to September, when swimming
  in the sea is banned by local authorities. Only swim at beaches with lifeguards and between
  their flags.

## Security
- Lock doors and close windows when you go out. Use the safe in the main bedroom for passports
  and valuables.
- Villas have CCTV at the gate and driveway only. There are no cameras inside the villas.
