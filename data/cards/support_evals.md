the OpenAI generation step. Generator: `data/generators/support_evals.py`, seed 7.

**Circularity:** the agent runs on Claude; question generation, the simulated user and the judge
run on OpenAI. **Splits:** `in_kb` (80) gets a fixed dev/test split; the others are under 30 and
are pass/fail with no split. **Personal data:** none — fictional business, invented guests,
`example.com` emails; canary phone/card numbers are public test values. **Licence:** repository.

**Real guest messages (OQ-5):** 32 questions were taken from the owner's own WhatsApp guest chats
(about 8,400 messages, 2025–26) and **rewritten**: names, phone numbers, emails, dates, prices,
the real properties' names and nearby landmarks were removed, and each question was re-set in
Palmgrove Stays terms while keeping the guest's wording style (typos, Hinglish, indirect asks).
The raw exports were processed on the owner's machine, never copied into this repository, and
the temporary extraction was deleted. Expected answers come from the Palmgrove KB, not from the
real conversations.

A second batch of 22 (17 in-KB, 5 out-of-KB, tagged "airbnb pre-arrival" in `notes`) came from the
owner's Airbnb message export (1,040 threads, ~1,100 distinct guest questions, mostly before
arrival), rewritten the same way. Only the messages file was read; the export's payment, ID and
profile files were deleted unread from the temporary workspace. Topics were picked to fill gaps
(parking, scooter rental, pets, children, Wi-Fi/power for remote work, trains and airports, house
rules, deposits); the most frequent real topics (early check-in, contact numbers, availability,
location) were already covered.
